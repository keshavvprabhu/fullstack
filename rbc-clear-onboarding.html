The Standard Document Package

Standing master set · agreed once, refreshed periodically
  • Master Account Agreement & RBC Clear T&Cs
  • Org chart & ownership structure
  • Beneficial ownership certification (re-confirmed each request)
  • Authorized signers: IDs & signature cards
  • SWIFT profile: BIC, RMA, MT selection
Per-account items · for each new entity / account
  • Account request (entity, currency, purpose)
  • Certificate of Formation; LPA / LLC Agreement
  • Good Standing cert; resolution & incumbency
  • IRS W-9 (EIN) or W-8BEN-E (non-US)
  • Expected activity & source of funds

Why repeatable works: FinCEN Order FIN-2026-R001 (Feb 13, 2026) lets RBC Clear rely on previously verified beneficial ownership data for new accounts once Blackstone certifies it is unchanged. Blackstone Inc. (NYSE-listed) is exempt from beneficial ownership collection; fund and SPV vehicles generally are not.
