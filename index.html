About Me

Keshav Prabhu is a seasoned product management professional with over a decade of experience in leading cross-functional teams to deliver innovative solutions. He has a proven track record of driving product strategy, enhancing user experience, and achieving business goals.

In addition to product leadership, Keshav maintains a strong background in Software Testing and Automation — designing test strategies, building automated test suites (unit, integration, and end-to-end), and implementing CI/CD testing pipelines to ensure reliable releases. He combines hands-on technical skills with domain knowledge to reduce defects, accelerate delivery, and improve product quality through data-driven testing and risk-based approaches.

Core Competencies

  • Product Strategy - Defining vision and roadmap for market success
  • Cross-functional Leadership - Aligning teams across engineering, design, and marketing
  • User Experience - Driving innovation focused on user needs
  • Business Goals - Delivering measurable results and revenue impact
  • Software Testing & Automation - Establishing test strategies, implementing automated test frameworks, and integrating testing into CI/CD to improve release quality and speed.
  • Technical & Domain Knowledge - Balanced expertise in system architecture, debugging, and domain-specific requirements to align technical solutions with business outcomes.
